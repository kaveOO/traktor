TRAKTOR · Build Plan v0.2 draft

Rów, który pracuje w obie strony (The ditch that works both ways) · KOWR "Zatrzymaj wodę, chroń plony" · HackNation 2026

1. In one line

For one real gmina, TRAKTOR maps every drainage ditch and finds where a board (weir) can hold water. It refuses unsafe sites and says why. Each day it tells the farmer TRZYMAJ (hold), OTWÓRZ (open) or NIE RUSZAJ (leave as is), and it prints a site card with the legal route.

4. How it works

Offline pipeline (Python, run once for the demo gmina) → static files

1 Clip ditches→2 Profile every 5 m from 1 m LiDAR→3 Channel visible?→4 Candidates every 100 m, skipping culverts→5 Backwater + m³ per crest + overflow fill→6 Refusals→7 Metrics (ranges)→8 Rank + export GeoJSON

Inputs: BDOT10k ditches/roads/buildings, NMT 1 m sheets, ULDK parcels, ISOK flood zones, IUNG soil drought. Outputs in public/data/traktor/: ditches, sites, parcels, profiles/{id}, summary. Target size under 5 MB.

Refusal reasons: road or culvert · building · flood zone · channel not visible · water spills onto a road or building · water spills onto a neighbour's parcel · slope too steep. Flags: buried drains unknown · owner unconfirmed · Natura 2000 not checked · sensitive downstream.

Order rules (the first match wins; thresholds are configurable)

  • OTWÓRZ if: IMGW rain warning at level 2 or above for the powiat · or at least 30 mm forecast in 72 h · or a hydro warning while the pool is more than half full
  • …but NIE RUSZAJ + "skonsultuj" (consult) if there is something sensitive downstream
  • TRZYMAJ if: KBW is −50 mm or lower, there are no warnings and it is April–September · or it is spring and dry
  • NIE RUSZAJ otherwise

Demo mode: DEMO_MODE=live|hybrid|fixtures. Fixtures are recorded from real API responses, so the whole demo runs with Wi-Fi off.

7. Honesty rules

  • Show ranges everywhere; every card says "wymaga wizji lokalnej" (requires a site visit)
  • A weir needs a pozwolenie wodnoprawne (water-law permit, art. 389 pkt 6)
  • No yield claims and no cost claims. Use only the cited −30.5% outflow and −33.6% nitrate
  • Scenarios, the KOWR overlay and manual fixes are always labelled as such
  • Credit the data sources on screen (IMGW, GUGiK, Open-Meteo, ISOK, IUNG, OSM)
